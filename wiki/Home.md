# Veskorius

A tech-progression mod for **NeoForge 1.21.1** built on one rule: **energy never travels
through a cable.** It fills a volume. A machine works if it stands inside an active field, and
stops when it doesn't.

> **Status: alpha 0.1.0.** All five tiers are playable end to end. Saves are not guaranteed
> across alpha versions.

## The short version

You start with nothing to manage. Dig between Y 0 and −20, find the veined stone sheathing a
pocket of raw crystal, and stabilise it by hand. Tier 1 is a crafting loop — no energy at all.

Then you find an outpost, wake its console, and it gives you the blueprint for a **Field
Emitter**. From there the mod is about volumes, bands, and upkeep: alloys and relays at T3,
amplifiers and automated extraction at T4, and at T5 the **Rift** — anchor it, ward it, extract
from it six times.

There is no wiki page that will teach you the mod better than the **Resonance Codex**, the
in-game guidebook you receive on your first login. It unlocks chapters as you earn them.
